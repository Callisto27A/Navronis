The 4 Canonical Injector Families

Streamlined to the 4 essential injector designs that power 99% of liquid rocket propulsion.

COAXIAL

Shear Coaxial

High-velocity annular gas shears a central liquid core. Momentum flux ratio J checked against the stable 2-20 window with Lorenzetto-Lefebvre SMD atomization.

Engines: SSME, RL10, Vulcain, Raptor
SWIRL

Centrifugal Swirl

Tangential inlet ports spin liquid into a centrifugal sheet around an air/gas core with coaxial gas shroud. Sized via Abramovich & Lefebvre sheet breakup theory.

Engines: RD-170, RD-180, NK-33
PINTLE

Central Pintle

Single central deflective shaft. Outer annular fuel gap deflected by radial LOX slots. Total Momentum Ratio (TMR) defines spray cone half-angle beta.

Engines: Apollo LMDE, Merlin 1D, TR-106
IMPINGING

Unlike Doublet

Discrete intersecting liquid jets forming an atomizing fan. Sized with Rupe momentum balance for uniform O/F distribution and Ingebo breakup SMD.

Engines: Apollo SPS, Titan II, Viking
